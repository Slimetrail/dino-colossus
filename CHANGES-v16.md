# Dino Colossus v16 — Continue?

- **5 hearts and 5 lives** at the start on every difficulty. Losing a life refills hearts to 5 (was 3).
- **Continue? screen** when you run out of lives: a big 20-second countdown (number + ring) and big YES / NO buttons.
  - **YES**: 5 new hearts and 5 new lives, same difficulty, same level/section, score kept. Unlimited continues.
  - **Died at a boss?** YES puts you back at the start of that same boss fight. The boss comes back at full health.
  - **NO, or the timer reaches 0**: the normal Game over screen, and if the score is good enough the usual Top 10 "pick your 3 letters" entry. Score is saved once.
- **Countdown rules**: it counts real seconds, but it pauses while the app is hidden (phone locked / switched apps) and resumes when you come back. The home button does nothing during the countdown, so the only ways out are YES, NO, or time running out. A double tap counts once, and after NO / time-up the run is over (no going back).
- Saves: no format change and no new save keys. Old v14/v15 saves and menu CONTINUE still work.
- Calm mode and mute unchanged. Service worker cache bumped to v16.
