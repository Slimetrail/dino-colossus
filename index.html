How to play

◀ ▶ to run (arrow keys / A D), JUMP (Space / W / ↑), POWER (E).

🧍 Gobble up cave people. Your dino gets fatter and fatter, and when nearly full it curls up and ROLLS (it speeds up, bounces, and jumps lower)! Run to a 🌋 volcano and BLEHHH them into the crater for a giant BOOM. They parachute down safely 🪂. Bigger loads = way more points.

Every dino eats the same way: gobble cave people, lizards, dodos, baby mammoths and giant bugs. Little dinos (and a rival who's smaller than you) can be eaten too. Eat the little-dino goal each section for a bonus. Bigger dinos and bosses can only be stomped. NOT edible: spear hunters, rocks, boulders and meteors.

Spear hunters and 🪨 boulders make you drop your load. Falling in a lava pit costs a ❤️ (you pop back out). You have 3 ❤️ per life and 3 LIVES. Lose all 3 lives and you pick a new dino for a fresh run.

Challenges: ☄️ meteor showers (dodge the red marks), 🔥 lava flows (jump over), 🦖 rival races (out-eat it). Every 5 sections a BOSS dino shows up. Jump on its head when it's dizzy!

✨ Pellets give a quick DASH. Amber ? blocks are surprises: usually good (dash, 💎, ❤️, +3 belly, invincible), sometimes silly-bad (shrink, backwards, burp, mini meteor). Hit a cracked fossil block from below to find a secret bonus room!

Each section ends at a giant volcano: barf in your whole load for a big bonus!

Difficulty: Hard has everything. Medium has no meteors. Easy has no meteors and ? blocks are always good. Each has its own high score.

Earn powers:

The run never ends. Every section is a new random place, and day turns to night as you play.

Game over!
