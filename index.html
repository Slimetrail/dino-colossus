How to play

🦖 Hi! You are a big, hungry dino!

◀ ▶ Tap the arrows to run. Tap JUMP to jump.

🧍 Eat cave people and little animals. Yum!

🍔 Eat a lot, and your belly gets FAT. You walk slow and waddle.

💨 Eat even more, and you curl into a ball. ZOOM! You roll super fast!

🌋 Run to a volcano. Your dino burps everyone in. BOOM! They float down safe. 🪂

🔥 Watch out for lava, rocks, and falling stars. Jump over them!

🗡️ Spear people are pointy! Do not jump on them. Jump OVER them.

👑 There are 20 boss dinos! Each one is a new dino, and each one is harder.

🔨 Grab the falling weapons and tap ATTACK. Or jump on its head when it is dizzy.

🏆 Beat a boss, then keep going. Beat all 20 to be the DINO CHAMPION!

▶ If you close the game, tap CONTINUE to go on after your last boss.

🏠 The house button stops the game and goes to the menu.

❓ Bump the ? blocks for a surprise.

🧱 Bonk a brick block with your head to break it!

❤️ You have 3 hearts and 3 lives.

🏆 Get a top score, and put in your name!

⚡ You can earn powers: speed, big belly, and fire breath!

Easy: no falling stars, ? blocks are always good. Medium: no falling stars. Hard: everything! Computer: arrow keys, Space to jump, E for power.
