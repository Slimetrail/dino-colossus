# Dino Colossus credits

Designed and created by SlimeTrail Productions

## Music (v19)
In game: "Designed and created by SlimeTrail Productions".

- `sounds/intro-elise-dino-variation.ogg` (title intro, plays once): our arrangement of the public-domain Für Elise motif.
- `sounds/dino-main-loop.ogg` (main play music, whole file loops).
- `sounds/dino-boss-loop.ogg` (boss music, whole file loops).
- `sounds/boss-beaten-fanfare.ogg` (plays once when a boss is beaten).

All four are original work released CC0, made with the CC0 VSCO-2 CE upright piano samples.
Licence texts are saved in `licences/` (OWN-WORK-CC0.txt, VSCO-2-CE-upright-CC0-1.0.txt). No credit is required.

## Sound effects (v13)
CC0 effects from Kenney (kenney.nl) and the SdiviHall Retro Monster Roar (OpenGameArt). No credit is required.
