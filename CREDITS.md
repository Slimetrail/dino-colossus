# Dino Colossus credits

## Music (v14)
In game: "Beethoven, Musopen, public domain".

- `sounds/music-pastoral.mp3` (main play music) is made from the Wikimedia Commons file
  "Ludwig van Beethoven - symphony no. 6 in f major 'pastoral', op. 68 - iii. allegro.ogg".
  The license section of the Commons page says "Music: {{PD-old-100}}" and "Recording: {{PD-author|Musopen}}",
  and the license short name is "Public domain". The source is Musopen.
- `sounds/music-minuet.mp3` (boss music) is made from the Wikimedia Commons file
  "Minuet in G (Beethoven), piano.ogg" (Minuet WoO 10 No. 2, played by Peter Johnston).
  The license section of the Commons page says "Music {{PD-old-100}}", "Recording {{PD-author|Peter Johnsto}}"
  and "{{Copyrighted free use}}", and the license short name is "Public domain". The source is Musopen.

Changes we made: mono, trimmed silence, peak at -6 dB, 1.5 s loop crossfade, 56 kbps mp3.
Musopen asks, as a courtesy, that people do not sell its recordings directly and that derived works credit Musopen.
